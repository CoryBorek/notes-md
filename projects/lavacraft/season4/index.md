We also now have a custom Resource Pack, of which I am the maintainer.

# Lore
I am based with Dysonaut this season. We work for a government organization called CRAFT (Crazy Redstoners and Farm Treaty).

CRAFT manages a lot of national parks, but sometimes uses their land for nefarious reasons.

We have two parks that we are going to build this season.

## CRAFT National Forest.
CRAFT National Forest is our starter base, in which we are building a lot of our infrastructure to build the main base.

The main base was planned months in advance, so this area is thrown together to make sure that it is possible to build the main base. It is custom-designed to house a lot of our farms, but also has a realistic-looking coastal forest.

Here, it was once a booming mining town, in which CRAFT was given the property to maintain and preserve, in which it does, but half of the land is also used for industrial purposes.

## CRAFT Mountain National Park.
This is our megabase this season. It is a large mountain far away from the spawn area in which we maintain and preserve the top for visitors alike, but have a hidden research facility within the inside of the mountain.

Inside the mountain will be two different minigames. One made by me, the other by Dyson. The entire project will be a huge undertaking, which is why we've been rushing the resource gathering for the first couple of months.
