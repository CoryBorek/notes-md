# LavaCraft Season 4 Music
About a year ago, I licensed a couple thousand songs to use in my content.

I sorted about 20 songs that I wanted to use this season out into a playlist.

I also added a couple instrumentals of a few of my own personal songs.

On top of that, I wrote two custom songs for this season specifically.

## Early Boating
This is a simple piano/guitar piece that I use on occasion when opening a regular video. It is especially used in opening timelapses.

## Snowstorm
This piece is my main theme for this season. While building the starter base, I am ending the videos with it. When I move on to working on the megabase, all timelapses in which I build the megabase will include this song. It is extremely cinematic, so I need to be careful about the shots that I use for it.
