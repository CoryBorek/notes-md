# Vectors
A Vector in 2 dimensions has 2 pieces of information.

1. Magnitude (length)
2. Direction (angle)

# Functions on Vectors
There are 4 different functions that we can do on a vector

## Scalar Multiplication

Let $v=(v_1,v_2,...v_n), v_1,v_2,...,v_n\in\R$ be a vector with n dimension. Let $c\in\R$. Then $cv=(cv_1,cv_2,...,cv_n)$.

## Addition

Let $u=(u_1,u_2,...,u_n)$ and $v=(v_1,...v_n)$ be vectors then $u+v=(u_1+v_1, u_2 + v_2,...,u_n+v_n)$

## Subtraction

Let $u=(u_1,u_2,...,u_n)$ and $v=(v_1,...v_n)$ be vectors then $u-v=(u_1-v_1, u_2-v_2,...,u_n-v_n)$
